## TARAflow 0.17.0-beta

IEC 62443-4-2 in TARAflow: the requirement matrix of your component — classic edition (2019) or the CRA edition EN IEC 62443-4-2 — derived from the TARA you already have.

> New project schema **12** — a project is converted when it is opened (a backup of the original is written next to it) and then no longer opens in 0.16.x. A TARA verification recorded with 0.16 stays valid.

### IEC 62443-4-2 and the Cyber Resilience Act

- **Two matrices, chosen by the tag:** `IEC 62443` for the classic edition with its device categories, the new tag `EN IEC 62443 (CRA)` for the CRA edition (draft prAA:2026, marked as draft). Both tags together: the CRA edition applies.
- **Compliance tab:** choose the component (or the whole model), set the SL-T per foundational requirement and see what the product reaches — SL-C from the model and the mitigations marked done, SL-C planned once the chosen mitigations are done, and the value with measures of the system around the component.
- **Derived, not ticked:** every row is checked against the DFD properties, the mitigations of the risk treatment (per element, done ones only for the achieved value) and supporting measures. An unset property is *unknown*, never *not applicable*. What the model cannot show gets a statement with reasoning and evidence; TARAflow tells you when the model changed since.
- **What to do next:** a to-do list up to the SL-T names per element the property to set or check, marks what the planned mitigations close and lists the missing statements. A quick guide walks through the steps.
- **What a requirement is about:** hover the number for title and summary, click it for everything — how TARAflow checks it, the elements concerned, fitting catalog mitigations, your statement. The summaries are in our own words; the standard is authoritative.
- **Report:** a new chapter with the security levels, the statement of applicability (with the source of every row), the CRA view by Annex I letter and the gaps with suggested mitigations — in every format and the CLI.

### DFD

- Processes and multiprocesses have **update mechanism** and **session control** (session lock, remote termination, session limit).
- A trust boundary can have **several traffic policies** — default deny, island mode, fail close — as IEC 62443 counts them separately.

### What you should do

1. Open your projects in 0.17 — they are converted to schema 12 (backup alongside).
2. For an IEC 62443 project set the tag `IEC 62443` or `EN IEC 62443 (CRA)` (Overview → tags), then open the Compliance tab and follow the quick guide.
3. **Azure DevOps** and the further tracker adapters come with 0.18; a project that depends on Azure DevOps should stay on 0.15.2.
