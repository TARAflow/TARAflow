## TARAflow 0.15.1-beta

Extensions of existing features: the hazard import now reads **EN ISO 12100 / EN ISO 13849-1 risk assessments** completely and can **re-import** a revised sheet; attack paths get **controls that fit their attack vector**; the **asset graph** shows hazard items and lets you move nodes as in draw.io.

> No new project schema — projects stay on **schema 10** and still open in 0.15.0-beta.

### Highlights

**Hazard import**
- Severity on the five-level person-harm scale, with a value-map editor for single columns.
- Default affected persons for sheets without a person column — without one, no severity reached a person.
- Lifecycle phase, hazard zone, protective measures and their type, risk graph S/F/P, PLr, PL achieved and safety function.
- The customer's rating is kept (probability, RPN, source values); a warning when the severity contradicts the risk graph.
- Hazard category suggested from free-text hazard types (German and English).
- **Re-import:** profiles are stored in the project; a revised sheet is recognised, column changes are followed, and rows are reconciled (new / changed / unchanged / removed) after a preview instead of being appended. Changed and removed hazards are flagged; nothing you edited is overwritten.

**Controls by attack vector**
- `av=network|adjacent|local|physical` on attack-tree nodes (ISO/SAE 21434 G.9), inherited down the tree.
- Attack-path risks get controls that fit the vector and the project's domain; 20 new catalog controls (firewall, RBAC/ABAC, SELinux/AppArmor, seccomp, SecOC, CAN IDS, signed OTA, SBOM patch management …).
- Attack potential and feasibility are not affected.

**Asset graph**
- Hazard items with their `contributes_to` / `endangers` edges, in the layer and the focus view; "Open in Hazard tab".
- Mouse as in draw.io: drag moves a node, Ctrl / ⌘ + drag pans. Side-column nodes snap to rows, including a free row above the top lane. Positions stay local, never in the project file.

**Fixes**
- The import no longer turns a missing severity into a minor injury and writes no fixed text into the project.
- Manually created assets (e.g. protection targets) now appear in the asset graph.
- Report: controls and verifications shown with their text; no empty per-interaction chapter in ISO projects.

### Upgrade notes
- No migration. A file saved with 0.15.1-beta opens and saves in 0.15.0-beta without losing the new data (import profiles, `av=`).
