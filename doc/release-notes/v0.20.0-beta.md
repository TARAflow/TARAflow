## TARAflow 0.20.0-beta

EN 50742 for both roles of a machine part, and guard rails on the way to stable results: the tag *EN 50742 B* opens the requirement set of a component's safety functions, EN 50742 relevance decides which interfaces and data flows the standard is about, and every audit commit now names all changes of the file.

> Project schema stays **14** — opening a project converts nothing. Results of existing **EN 50742 A** projects can change (see *What you should do*); other projects keep their results.

### EN 50742 Approach B

- **Tag *EN 50742 B*** (prEN 50742:2025, component role): a requirement matrix with the IEC 62443-4-2:2019 requirements of Table 4 for the component's safety functions at a fixed SL-T (FR1–FR3, FR7: 2; FR5, FR6: 1). Variants Table 4 does not name are shown as recommended, not counted. The EN 50742 clauses of Clauses 4, 6, 8 and 9 and the system role as a statement complete it.
- The tag brings *IEC 62443* along; the two matrices share their statements.
- New data store field **Audit records kept** (only for stores holding audit logs), for 8.5 and for Approach A 7.3.4.
- The risks keep the standard factors, the attacker's motive included: R = L × I is the cyber risk, the safety side of B is the fixed requirement set.

### EN 50742 relevance (A and B)

- **Safety zone:** mark a trust boundary as *Safety zone (EN 50742)*. Every flow crossing its border and every interface such a flow passes is relevant. Without a zone, the interfaces and flows on a path to a safety function are. Imported components are judged by the elements the import put into the machine.
- **In Approach A** only the relevant risks get exposure level, attacker capability, window of opportunity and an SRSL; the others are rated as usual, and the risk dialog says why. Attack-path risks are relevant through the steps that name a relevant interface or flow.
- **SRSL 0 only for an isolated safety function** — every interface on a path to it has EL0. Otherwise its risks get at least SRSL 1.
- **Safety requirements stay:** accepting a risk or prioritising it *won't* lifts no SRSL control; such a risk stays open in the progress. A threat that does not apply is dismissed at the threat as *not relevant* — on a relevant interface with a note. Row 7.1 looks at every threat of an exposed interface.
- **Risk register:** a column *Safety* and the filter *Only safety-relevant*.
- **One base method:** EN 50742 A adds to the standard method or ETSI TVRA; the Methodology view names both (*ETSI TVRA + EN 50742 Approach A*). Combinations that cannot go together replace each other when a tag is added; stored tags are never rewritten.
- **The rules on one page:** `doc/InProgress/RegulationCompliance/en-50742-approach-a-design.md` §0 lists what TARAflow decides in an EN 50742 A project and which of it the standard asks for.

### In the diagram

- **Exposure levels** of boundaries and interfaces as a small text, the **safety zone** with a yellow, dashed frame — drawn by the app, never stored: opening a project changes nothing, and a component's content hash does not depend on them. The page images show them, the report explains them in a legend. A project setting turns the texts on or off; by default they are on in EN 50742 projects.

### Also new

- **Threat catalogue version:** each (re)generation records the catalogue version per method. A newer catalogue is named in the Threats tab and phase, with the regeneration and its preview one click away; the Methodology view and the report name both versions.
- **Audit commits name every change of the file:** a threat's relevance, workflow and threat actor and a risk's treatment, justifications and chosen verifications are lines of the threat or risk itself (*Relevance: unrated → relevant*); every other change is listed by record as the analyst knows it (`threats.perElement[DB-E-1].causeDescription: … → …`), sorted, at most 20 per commit. Values the app computes, the layout, the method a tab shows and the report settings stay out; a token or password is never shown. The diff now also compares the threats of both methods and notices a measure replaced by another of the same status.
- **DFD info while rating:** an ⓘ next to each element and data flow in the threat tables, and right after its name in the threat and risk dialogs, shows what the DFD knows about it — properties, boundaries, flows, linked assets.
- **Change the prefix of an imported component instance** (*Change prefix…* in the multiprocess's tab *Component* and in Overview → Components): every id of the instance follows, the decisions on it stay. The import no longer proposes a prefix the component uses inside.
- **Issue tracker:** tracker and address are project settings, saved as changed and named in the audit; *Disconnect* only ends the connection on this machine and keeps tracker, address, project and ticket mapping. Changing them while connected asks first.
- **Threats no longer generated:** the *Keep / Remove* dialog shows the threat's text.
- **Why risks are still open:** the status chip of the Risks tab and the Risk phase name the reasons and the risks concerned.
- **Imported component boundaries carry the instance prefix** (*[SD1:CASE]*, *[C1:SD1:CASE]* for nested imports).
- **Window title** names the project and its file.
- **Warning before a tag change deletes risk ratings**, naming the factors and the number of ratings.
- **Git:** read-only channels accept read-only commands only; the SSH key field of the Audit settings is used for the push; a personal access token is no longer written into the remote URL.
- **Examples:** *EN50742-A-SafetyZone* and *EN50742-B-SafetyZone* in TARAflow_Examples.

### Fixed

- Nested component imports no longer read their element ids back wrong after the save.
- Threat and risk text no longer shows an i18n key; such text is repaired when a project is opened.
- The Methodology view names the EN 50742 approach next to ETSI TVRA.
- *Disconnect* in the Issue tracker tab no longer removes the ticket mapping, and the audit commit shows no tracker credentials.
- The assets of an imported component no longer lose their DFD links on a DFD update; opening the Assets tab no longer changes a machine with an imported component.
- "Safety Factor Source Removed" no longer asks while the safety data is there.
- The Risks tab follows a threat confirmed in the Threats tab (no "uncertain" left over); a changed relevance asks for a risk update.
- Report: the DFD figure follows the EL-badge setting; a *won't* risk stays in the SRSL chapter.
- EN 50742: 7.3.4 / 8.5 are met by a store keeping the audit logs ≥ 5 years outside the scope; the scope chip names the safety zone.
- Threat dialog: ✓ / ✗ when editing a text, the buttons below disabled meanwhile.

### What you should do

1. **A token in a remote URL:** a personal access token an earlier push wrote into the remote URL is removed on the next push — **revoke it** at the provider.
2. **EN 50742 A projects:** update the risks (sync banner in the Risks tab) — stored SRSL values that no longer fit are corrected then; until then the requirement matrix shows them as a gap. Expect new open rows: risks prioritised *won't* stay in the SRSL rows, 7.1 looks at every threat of an interface, and risks outside the safety zone lose their SRSL.
3. **Mark the safety zone** in your EN 50742 projects — without a zone or a linked safety function the model rows stay *unknown*.
4. **Threats dismissed as *not relevant* on a relevant interface without a note** are listed as warnings in the Threats tab; add the reason.
5. **Machines with imported components from 0.19:** *Check* in the multiprocess's tab *Component* names an instance an older TARAflow inserted and offers **Refresh stored state…** — also when the component is unchanged; it adds the instance prefix to the boundaries and corrects the ids of nested imports. The audit then shows the new display ids once. A prefix you do not like: **Change prefix…**.
6. **Components with threat text stored as an i18n key** are repaired when opened; a machine that imported such a component reports a newer state of it once.
7. **Tag changes that switch off risk factors** delete their ratings — the app now asks first; a committed project can be restored from the repository.
8. **The first save after the update** may write two default values (regulation preset, likelihood method); the audit lists them as derived values.
