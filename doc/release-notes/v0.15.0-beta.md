## TARAflow 0.15.0-beta

ISO/SAE 21434 projects now get the **risk value 1–5** defined by the standard, together with threat scenarios, a review list for treatment decisions and a methodology chapter in the report. In addition, the attack-tree likelihood is corrected for projects on a 3- or 4-level risk scale, in every preset.

> ⚠️ **Risk values change.** Opening a project converts it to **schema 10**. TARAflow asks first and keeps a backup of the original. Please review the values afterwards (see *Upgrade notes*).

### Highlights

**ISO/SAE 21434**
- **Risk value 1–5** (clause 15.8): impact × attack feasibility, read from the matrix in Annex H.8. It is shown with a word — Minimal / Low / Medium / High / Critical — and replaces I × L in the register, filter, heat map (the 4 × 4 H.8 matrix), risk dialog and report.
- **Threat scenarios:** the attack paths of one tree form one scenario. Its value and residual are the highest of its paths.
- **Review list:** Retain at a value of 4 or more and Reduce at a value of 1 are listed in a banner. You can filter the register to these risks. Nothing changes automatically.
- **ISO wording:** the treatments are named Avoid / Reduce / Share / Retain, and the impact levels Negligible / Moderate / Major / Severe on the 4-level scale.
- **Traceable calculation:** every ISO risk records which method and tables it was computed with.

**Report**
- New **Methodology** chapter (ISO): method, attack-potential bands, attack-tree gate rules, the H.8 matrix, treatment names, cybersecurity properties and calculation versions.
- New **Risk Assessment (Threat Scenarios)** chapter (ISO).
- The **Attack Trees** chapter is now rendered in every preset. It was previously empty. It shows the structure with AND/OR gates and leaf ratings, and the paths with attack potential, feasibility and relevance.
- The table-of-contents links in HTML and Markdown work again. Deselected chapters no longer appear in HTML or in the PDF.
- `taraflow-report` adds chapters that were introduced after a project was saved.

**Fixes**
- **Attack-tree likelihood on 3- and 4-level risk scales** (all presets): a *high* path used to give likelihood 5, above the top of the scale. It is now mapped onto the project's scale. For example, a risk of 12.5 becomes 10.
- A risk sync without a change no longer marks the project as modified.
- An attack tree whose stored structure no longer matches its DSL is parsed again when the project is opened.

### Upgrade notes
- **Schema 10:** earlier TARAflow versions cannot open converted files.
- **Values to review:**
  - ISO projects now show risk values 1–5 instead of I × L; the review list points to decisions worth a second look.
  - Projects with attack trees on a 3- or 4-level risk scale get corrected I × L values.
  - Treatments, priorities and justifications stay unchanged.
- Schema 10 files saved by a development build before this release are not migrated again. Set `schemaVersion` to `9` in the file before opening it; it is safe to run the migration a second time.
